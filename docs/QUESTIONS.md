
# Fraud First Aid – Project Questions

These questions define the core problems that Fraud First Aid must solve.

## 1. Emergency response

What should a victim do immediately after discovering an online financial fraud?

## 2. Reporting channels

Where should a victim report a cyber financial fraud in India?

## 3. 1930 helpline

When should a victim call the 1930 cybercrime helpline, and what information should they provide?

## 4. Cybercrime portal

How can a victim submit a financial cybercrime complaint through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal?

## 5. Bank notification

Why should a victim immediately inform their bank about a suspicious or fraudulent transaction?


## 6. Unanswerable question

What was the exact password used by the victim when registering their bank account?

## 7. Reporting deadlines

How does the time taken to notify the bank affect the customer's liability?

## 8. Scam classification

How can the application distinguish between UPI fraud, OTP theft, phishing, fake-job scams and other online financial frauds?

## 9. Evidence collection

What evidence should victims preserve, such as transaction IDs, screenshots, phone numbers, messages and complaint acknowledgements?

## 10. Complaint escalation

What can a victim do if their bank does not resolve their complaint within the applicable time limits?

---

## Purpose

These 10 questions will guide the application's features, test cases and evidence-based responses.

All legal and regulatory answers must be verified against current official sources.
